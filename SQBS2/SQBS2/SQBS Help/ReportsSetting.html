Reports Settings

By default, the first seven listed pages will be generated in web reports with the listed suffixes appended to the saved name. Pages can be included or excluded by selecting checkboxes, and the suffixes can be altered in the text fields.

Note that the suffixes are standard for most published reports, so altering names could be confusing for a viewer that may navigate by entering URLs rather than by clicking hyperlinks.

Finally, the “British Style Reports” option changes references to “tossups” with “starters”.
