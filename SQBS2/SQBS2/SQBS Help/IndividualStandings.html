Individual Standings

The Team Standings report can be selected through the “Reports” menu or by pressing ⌘I (if not currently in the “Reports” tab, the tab will be selected). Players are not separated by division, but if they are used, the player’s division will appear beside their name. Players are sorted by points per game unless the Pts/TUH option is checked under “Sorting” in the Settings tab. Depending on what options were selected when the tournament was created, the possible statistic headings are:
GP:Games played
10, etc:Number of responses for each different point value
TUH:Tossups heard
P/TU:Points per tossup heard
P/N:Ratio of powers (first column) to negs (third column)
G/N:Ratio of gets (all columns except third) to negs
Pts:Points earned by the player
PPG:Points per game
