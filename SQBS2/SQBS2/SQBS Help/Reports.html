Reports

There are three quick reports available on the “Reports” tab. To select one, use the drop–down menu labeled “Select Quick Reports”. Additionally, reports can be generated by selecting them through the Reports menu or using their corresponding hotkeys. Generated reports appear in the blank space below the drop–down menu. To print the report (or save to PDF), click the “Print” button. Before printing, the table can be condensed to fit on one page (if necessary) by selecting the checkbox.
