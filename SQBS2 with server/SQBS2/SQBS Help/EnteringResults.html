Entering Scoring Data

Starting with the team score field of Team A, applicable numbers can be entered based on scoresheet information.

Tabbing between fields accesses, in order: Team A score, Team B score, Team A players’ powers (if applicable), normal tossups and negs (individual points are calculated automatically), Team A bonus and lightning information (if applicable), Team B players’ powers, normal tossups and negs, Team B bonus and lightning information (if applicable), the round number, the game ID (goes up incrementally by 1 by default), the tossups heard (if applicable), and the “Save” button.

Note that some fields only show up via tabbing if “All Controls” option is selected in Keyboard / Keyboard Shortcuts System Preferences on your Mac.
